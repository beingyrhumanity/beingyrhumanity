Our mission

Tonight, how many children will go to sleep on an empty stomach?

How it works

Four simple steps — no long process, no middlemen.

01

Join the team

Fill the form, get added to our group.

02

Contribute monthly

Give whatever amount you can comfortably afford, every month.

03

Funds are pooled

Everyone's contribution comes together into one common fund.

Donated to an NGO
